White Sauce Pasta

Creamy, rich, and delicious white sauce pasta is the ultimate comfort food made with garlic, butter, milk, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces penne or fettuccine pasta
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups warm milk
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente, then drain.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat; sauté minced garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Sprinkle in the flour and whisk continuously for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Slowly pour in warm milk while whisking to prevent lumps until the sauce thickens.
  5. Stir in Parmesan cheese, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper until smooth and creamy.
  6. Toss the cooked pasta into the white sauce, mix well, and serve hot!
