Apple Pancakes

These apple pancakes are perfect for a weekend breakfast or brunch. One batch is never enough for my family! I recommend eating these pancakes with hot cinnamon syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar or to taste
  • 1 ¼ teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon or to taste
  • 1 cup milk or as needed
  • ¼ cup butter, melted
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup shredded tart apple

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Sift flour, sugar, baking powder, and cinnamon together into a large bowl.
  3. Make a well in the center and add milk, melted butter, and egg; mix until smooth. Stir shredded apple into pancake batter until just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat. Pour or scoop batter onto the hot griddle, using about 1/4 cup per pancake; cook until bubbles form and the edges are dry, 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Flip and cook until browned on the other side. Repeat with remaining pancake batter.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
